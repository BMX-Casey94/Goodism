Maintain Sovereignty over a New Born – Info Pack
Asserting owner’s rights to a new-born
Many people would find it hard to believe, but there is no obligation to register the
birth of a baby. All you need is evidence of its live birth. You will need to know the full
name in advance of the birth and an open-minded mid-wife. The following information
is based on a real-life experience reported on social media:
Ask the midwife for the Live Birth Certificate and ask for the notes for the Labour and
the Birth. The family were given:
– Baby Birth Record
– Birth Record Transferred to Post-Natal Community Care Letter
• If the above is not practicable (as in the case of an NHS midwife sent to a home
birth), get a Doula, Birthkeeper, or independent witness to make a signed
declaration along the following lines:
"I, [ Doula or Birthkeeper's full name], hereby declare and confirm
that I was present on [insert day of week, day of month and year] at
[insert time] at the address known to me as [insert address in lower
case and post-code in square brackets) when the mother known to
me as [insert your full name] successfully gave live birth to a baby
boy whom I now know to be [First Name(s) Last Name]"
Signature:
Date:
Relationship to the Mother:
[Ideally handwritten in blue ink and signed in blue ink]

Before she left, the family asked the Midwife to sign a Midwife Witness Statement
to confirm that she attended the birth with the time and date with her midwife
registration number.
If you give birth in a hospital, make sure you get the Certificate of Live Birth before
you leave the hospital and take your Midwife Witness Statement to be signed on
the day of the birth.
Then send a Declaration of Denial of Consent to Register Children, signed by
the mother and the father, to their local Registration Office and to the main office in
Southport.
NHS England will send a Registration Confirmation Letter with the baby’s NHS number
after you register the baby with a GP.
Can an unregistered baby get a passport?
The 11-month-old baby of the family featured above got her first British passport with
no Birth Certificate/Certified Copy of An Entry. Throughout the process both mother
and father were repeatedly told by the Passport Office that the baby needed a Birth
Certificate to get a passport.
During their first phone call with the Passport Office, they were told that it would be
impossible to get the baby a British Passport without a Birth Certificate, even with a
Live Birth Certificate, however, they stood their ground and sought advice.
They were advised to send a Declaration of Denial of Consent to Register
Children, along with copies of all the above documents to the Passport Office
together with a Statutory Declaration, giving reasons as to why the baby was not
registered. A sample template declaration is set out below.

Monumental recent ruling from the USA
District Judge TREVOR N. McFADDEN ruled on July 29th 2025 that no Social Security
Number, Birth Certificate, or Letter of No Record (the equivalent of our letter/notice
of denial of consent to register) can be required for issuance of a passport. The point
is that neither the NI number (equivalent to the US Social Security no.,) nor the birth
certificate are evidence of identity. The passport only becomes evidence of identity
when we add our photograph and sign it with our unique autograph. In the case of a
baby, the identification comes from the witnessed and attested fact of being born live
by a midwife or doula and the mother or father’s corroboration that the baby born live
is theirs to name and take responsibility for.
Considering that passports are issued by the Post Office, it is not even a governmental
process. It is a procedure for international jurisdiction under UPU (Universal Postal
Union) rules. For greater detail on the judicial ruling please go to:
https://passagetoliberty.substack.com/p/monumental-ruling-district-judge
What about child benefit?
Once you get the baby a passport, you can then apply for and get child benefit. When
you apply for Child Benefit you send off a copy of the Passport and get the benefit
back dated. You will need to complete and sign a Statutory Declaration like the
one above.
